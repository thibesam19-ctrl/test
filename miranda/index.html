Our Story

Built on a belief that every woman deserves to feel beautiful

Miranda was born from a simple truth: self-care is not a luxury — it's a language of self-love. We curate products, experiences and rituals that honour the woman you already are, while nurturing the one you're becoming.

Every product in our collection is thoughtfully sourced, clean in formulation and beautiful in purpose. We believe what you put on your skin — and into your home — should be as intentional as the life you're building.

— Miranda, Founder
