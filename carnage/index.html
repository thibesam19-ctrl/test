The Philosophy

We don't
tame it.

Carnage was born on a ridge above the treeline where the wind doesn't care who you are. We make gear for that ridge — not for the parking lot.

Every stitch, every material, every silhouette is stress-tested in conditions that would snap anything designed in a studio. We design in the field, fail in the field, and rebuild until it's right.

Nothing is decorative here. If it doesn't serve a function, it isn't on the garment.

The Build

No
shortcuts.
Ever.

01
Welded seams
Ultrasonic bonding eliminates stitching as a failure point. 10,000mm HH rating, tested over 5,000 abrasion cycles.
02
Reclaimed face fabric
Every shell is woven from post-consumer PET. Reclaimed, not recycled into something weaker.
03
YKK Aquaguard hardware
Every zip, cord-lock, and buckle rated for submersion. Standard across the range — no exceptions.
04
Field-repairable design
Modular baffles, replaceable gaskets. You can rebuild this in a tent with a multitool.

What We Use

Materials
sourced honestly.

We publish every material's origin, mill, and impact data. No greenwashing. If we don't know exactly where something comes from, it isn't on our gear.

Recycled Nylon
100% post-consumer. Woven in Biella, Italy. Carries the same tensile rating as virgin 70D — verified by SGS.
PFC-Free DWR
Durable water repellency without fluorochemicals. Bio-based. Rated 90/100 on the OEKO-TEX® Bluesign index.
Merino Wool
18.5 micron. ZQ-certified flocks in New Zealand. Non-mulesed. Live fibre tracking from paddock to garment.
Down Alternative
Primaloft® Gold Active+. Performs when wet, packs to 650-fill-equivalent. Entirely animal-free.
Vibram® Outsole
Megagrip compound with 53% recycled natural rubber. Tested on limestone, wet basalt, and scree at angle.
Dyneema® Core
15× stronger than steel at equal weight. Used in critical load paths in the Scree Pack and harness webbing.
